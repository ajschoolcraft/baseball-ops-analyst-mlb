### Statcast Metrics Context

  

How much do our selected underlying bat tracking and quality of contact matter to tangible statistics and performance at the plate? This page allows
you to compare those outcomes.

* **Barrel:** For a batted ball, the ideal combination of exit velocity & launch angle. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/barrel).
* **Blast:** A swing that is an ideal combination of squared-up and with strong bat speed. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/bat-tracking-blasts).
* **Fast Swing:** A swing that has 75 mph or greater bat speed. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/fast-swing-rate).
* **Hard-Hit:** A batted ball hit with 95 mph or greater exit velocity. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/hard-hit-rate).
* **Ideal Attack Angle:** A batted ball hit with an [attack angle](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/attack-angle) between 5° and 20°. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/ideal-attack-angle).
* **Launch Angle Sweet-Spot:** A batted ball hit with launch angle between 8° and 32°. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/sweet-spot).
* **Squared-Up:** A swing where at least 80% of the potential exit velocity (based on swing speed and pitch speed) is converted into actual exit velocity. Read more [here](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/squared-up).

---

**Note:**  Barrels, Hard-Hit Rate and Launch Angle Sweet-Spot % metrics have data from **2016-present**. Blasts, Fast Swing, Ideal Attack Angle and Squared-Up metrics have data from **2023-present** (2023 bat tracking data is partial).
