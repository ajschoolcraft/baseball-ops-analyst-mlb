# Tell Me About Yourself — Draft v1

I'm AJ Schoolcraft, graduating from LMU this May with a B.S. in Information Systems and Business Analytics. My coursework has been heavily technical — SQL, Python, Tableau, machine learning — giving me a strong quantitative foundation for this role.

I don't just learn these tools in class, I build with them. For my capstone I created an AI-powered presentation coach as a mobile web app, and on my own I built an end-to-end MLB analytics pipeline — Stats API and Statcast extraction, dbt transformations, a Snowflake warehouse, and a Streamlit dashboard.

My internship at Murow Development Consultants gave me hands-on experience with data at scale — building financial models tracking $12 million in reimbursements and auditing hundreds of line items to identify half a million in eligible recoveries. That's the same muscle: digging through messy data, finding the signal, getting the numbers right.

On a personal level, I've been a baseball fanatic my whole life — Mariners fan, going to spring training and tracking player stats since I was two. I have a natural feel for numbers and a real drive to work in this industry.

That's why building an MLB analytics project was a no-brainer — turning raw performance data into visual insights. Honestly, the only surprise is I didn't narrow it down to just the Mariners.
